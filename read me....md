# &#x20;                                      **LDR SENSOR MODULE.**

#### &#x20;    **1. PROJECT DESCRIPTION**

###### &#x20;               **An LDR module detects the intensity of light and gives an electrical output based on the surrounding light level.**   



#### &#x20;    **2. OBJECTIVE**

###### &#x20;               **\* To detect light intensity.**

###### &#x20;               **\* To understand LDR sensor operation.**

###### &#x20;               **\* To  control an LED based on light conditions.**

#### &#x20;    **3.  COMPONENTS REQUIRED**

###### &#x20;               **\* LDR module.**

###### &#x20;               **\* LED.**

###### &#x20;               **\* Bread board.**

###### &#x20;               **\* Jumper wires.**

###### &#x20;               **\* Battery/DC supply.**

###### &#x20;               **\* Resistor.**

#### &#x20;     **4. WORKING PRINCIPLE**

###### &#x20;                   **The LDR resistance changes with light intensity.**

###### &#x20;                   **\*More light - lower resistance.**

###### &#x20;                   **\* Less light - higher resistance.**

###### &#x20;                                  **The module produces an output signal, which controls the LED.**

#### &#x20;      **5. CONSTRUCTION PROCESS**

###### &#x20;               **\* Place the LDR module on the breadboard.**

###### &#x20;               **\* Connect power and ground.**

###### &#x20;               **\* Connect the output to the LED circuit.**

###### &#x20;               **\* Connect the battery.**

###### &#x20;               **\* Change the light falling on the LDR and observe the LED.**

#### &#x20;      **6. APPLICATIONS**

###### &#x20;               **\* Automatic street light.**

###### &#x20;               **\* Night lamps.**

###### &#x20;               **\* Light detection systems.**

###### &#x20;               **\* Security systems.**

###### &#x20;               **\* Automatic lighting.**

#### &#x20;       **7. LEARNING OUTCOMES**

###### &#x20;                **\* Understand LDR operation.**

###### &#x20;                **\* Learn sensor interfacing.**

###### &#x20;                **\* Learn breadboard wiring.**

###### &#x20;                **\* Understand basic automation.**

#### &#x20;       **8. PROJECT CATEGORY**

###### &#x20;                 **\* Electronics - sensor - based automation.**
