### &#x20;                                                                        **GREETING CARD**  

#### &#x20;                       **1 . PROJECT DESCRIPTION**

###### &#x20;                                      **This is an interactive handmade greeting card using an LED ,aluminium sheet contacts and a CR 2032 lithium coin cell battery .When the card is opened, the LED turns ON . When the card is closed , the LED turns OFF.**

#### &#x20;                       **2 . OBJECTIVE**

###### &#x20;                                      **\* Create a interactive handmade greeting card.**

###### &#x20;                                      **\* Develop creativity and paper - craft skills.**

###### &#x20;                                      **\* Learn cutting ,folding, drawing and decorative techniques.**

###### &#x20;                                      **\* To turn an LED ON when the card is opened.**

###### &#x20;                                      **\* To turn the LED OFF when the card is closed.**

###### &#x20;                                      **\* To learn the use of aluminium contacts as a simple switch.**          

#### &#x20;                        **3 . COMPONENTS REQUIRED**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* Chart/ craft paper.**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* Colour paper.**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* Scissors.**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* Glue.**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* Pencil and sketch pens.**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* Decorative materials.**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* LED.**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* Aluminium sheet.**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* CR 2032 lithium coin cell battery.** 

#### &#x20;                        **4 . WORKING PRINCIPLE**

###### &#x20;                                        **The aluminium sheets work as a electrical contacts. When the card is opened, the contacts complete the circuit and current flows from the CR 2032 lithium coin cell battery through the LED, so the LED glows. When the card is closed, the contacts separate or break the circuit , so the LED turns OFF.**

#### &#x20;                         **5 .  CONSTRUCTION PROCESS**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* Prepare and fold the greeting card.**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* Fix the LED inside the card.**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* Fix aluminium sheet at the required contact points.**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* Connect the LED and aluminium contacts to the CR 2032 lithium coin cell battery.**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* Adjust the contact position so the LED turns ON when the card is opened.**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* Test the card by opening and closing it.**

#### &#x20;                         **6 . APPLICATIONS**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* Interactive greeting cards.**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* Birthday and festival cards.**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* Electronic paper- craft projects.**

###### &#x20;                                       **\* Basic electronics demonstrations.**

#### &#x20;                         **7 . LEARNING OUTCOMES**

###### &#x20;                                        **\* Learned basic LED circuit connections.**

###### &#x20;                                        **\* Understand how electrical contacts can work as a switch.**

###### &#x20;                                        **\* Learned to use a CR 2032 lithium coin cell battery in a small electronic project.**

###### &#x20;                                        **\* Learned to combine electronics with creative paper craft.**

#### &#x20;                         **8 . PROJECTIVE CATEVGORY**

###### &#x20;                                         **Electronics + creative design / interactive paper craft .**
